THE WORD MIRROR: DISCOVERING GOD, DEFINING YOU

Texts: James 1;-5; 2 Corinthians 3:18; John 1:1-18

INTRODUCTION

Before a man can truly approach God, he must first be confronted with the reality of his own condition-and the Old Testament Tabernacle was divinely designed to make this encounter unavoidable. Far more than a structure for worship, it served as a spiritual blueprint revealing how God relates with humanity (Exodus 25:8-9), Its four sections-the Gate, the Outer Court, the Holy Place, and the Holy of Holies-represents progessive stages of access to God, moving from entry to intimate communion.

Within the Outer Court, three elements ere central to israel's worship experience. The Brazen altar symbolised sacrifice and atonement (Leviticus 17:11), establishing that reconcilation ith God requires the shedding of blood. The Brazen Laver represented cleansing (Exodus30:18-21), emphasising the necessity of purity before service. The Table of Shewbread pointed to devine provision and fellowship (Leviticus 24:5-9), reminding Israel of God's sustaning presence. Among these, the Brazen Laver carries profound spiritual meaning. Made from polished mirrors (Exodus 38:8), it functioned both as a reflection and a cleansing vessel. As the priest looked into it, he was cleansed. It therefore severed a dual purpose: revelation and purification. This is symbolic of the Word of God.

Like the laver, the Word reveals God's nature while exposing the true condition of the believer. More than that, it provides the means for transformation, guiding "Word Mirror" performs two vital functions: it reveals God to man and defines man before God. In this way, the Tabernacle-especially the Brazen Laver-remains a timeless model of how divine revelation leads to cleansing, transformation and deeper fellowship with God.

Without the Word, God would remain unknown, misunderstood and misrepresented. The Word is God's self revelation: "In the biginning was the Word and the Word was with God, AND THE WORD WAS WITH GOD. Ruby is a precious red variety of the mineral corundum (), colored by chromium, and is one of the most valuable colored stones, with prices increasing significantly for high-quality, larger, and "pigeon blood" red stones. As a November birthstone, it is often associated with wisdom, clarity, and strength. It is primarily sourced from Brazil, with natural colors ranging from colorless to yellow, brown, and rare pink or red, while most vibrant blues are heat-treated.

    3. Diamonds

      4. Diamond is a rare natural mineral composed of carbon known for its extreme hardness, high thermal conductivity, and unique optical properties like intense sparkle and brilliance. It is one of the four traditional "precious stones," along with rubies, sapphires, and emeralds.

    4. Beryls

      5. Beryl is a durable, highly prized mineral gemstone () ranging from 7.5 to 8 on the Mohs scale, known for forming hexagonal crystals in colors like green (emerald), blue (aquamarine), pink (morganite), and yellow (heliodor). Primarily sourced from Brazil, Colombia, and Africa, it is used in jewelry and is the main ore of beryllium.

    5. Onyx

      6. Onyx is a silica based chalcedony mineral known for its parallel, contrasting bands of color, commonly deep black or white. It is a semi-translucent, durable stone (on the Mohs scale) often used in jewelry, decorative items, and backlight interior design elements like countertop or walls. It is distinct from banded calcite/limestone, often called "Mexican Onyx".

    6. Jasper

      7. Jasper is an opaque, microcrystalline variety of quartz (chalcedony) known for its vibrant, varied, and often patterned appearance, ranging in colors from red, yellow, and green to brown and black. With a Mohs hardness of 6.5–7, it is durable, often used in jewelry as cabochons, and prized for grounding and protective metaphysical properties.
